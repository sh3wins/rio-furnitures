# RIO — Photo & video list

Landscape = wider than tall. Shoot in daylight where possible, no heavy filters.
Send the originals; they get resized for the web before going on the site.
Name files simply, e.g. `restaurant-room.jpg`, `chair-04-front.jpg`.

## 1. Rooms — the most important (8 photos)
One wide, landscape photo of a real space furnished with RIO furniture, for each:
- Restaurants
- Bars & Cafés
- Offices
- Schools
- Churches
- Hospitality (hotel / Airbnb / guest room)
- Outdoor
- Other (any other commercial space)
Tip: shoot from a low, straight-on angle so the furniture is clearly visible.
Nice to have: 2–3 extra angles of each room.

## 2. Products (per product, 3–6 photos)
On a plain light wall/floor, same spot and light for every product:
- Front

- Rear
- Close-up detail (joint, leg, edge)
- Material close-up (wood grain, fabric, metal)
- One in a real room
Plus: one photo of each available finish/colour (a swatch or the piece in that finish).

Materials · Cutting · Fabrication · Assembly · Finishing ·
Quality control · Packaging · Delivery

## 4. Real projects (per finished project, 3+ photos)
- 1 wide photo of the finished space
- 2+ detail photos
And these facts: what the customer was building, what RIO supplied
(product + quantity + finishes), materials, anything custom,
and a one-line headline (e.g. "A 42-seat restaurant in Westlands").

lead time, and a price or "quote only".
